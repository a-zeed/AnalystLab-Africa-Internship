# 🍎 Apple Inc. (AAPL) Historical Stock Market Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes five years of Apple Inc. (AAPL) historical stock market data using Python. The objective is to demonstrate advanced data transformation, time-series analysis, and feature engineering techniques to uncover trends in stock performance and generate actionable insights.

This project was completed as part of **Week 6** of the **AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship Program**.

---

# 📂 Dataset

**Source:** Yahoo Finance

**Dataset:** Apple Inc. (AAPL) Historical Stock Prices

The dataset contains:

* Date
* Open Price
* High Price
* Low Price
* Close Price
* Adjusted Close Price
* Trading Volume

# 💡 Key Insights

* Apple demonstrated strong long-term stock growth over the five-year period.
* Trading volume remained consistently high, indicating strong market participation.
* Moving averages highlighted a sustained upward market trend despite short-term fluctuations.
* Monthly returns revealed periods of both positive and negative performance, reflecting changing market conditions.
* Rolling volatility showed moderate levels of price variability throughout the analysis period.
