# 📊 Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance using the Superstore Sales Dataset and Microsoft Power BI. The goal was to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights through interactive visualizations, KPI analysis, and data storytelling.

The dashboard provides decision-makers with a clear view of revenue, profitability, customer behavior, and regional performance.

---

## Business Objective

To identify key revenue and profit drivers, evaluate sales performance across regions and product categories, and support data-driven business decisions through interactive reporting.

---

## Dataset

**Dataset:** Superstore Sales Dataset

The dataset contains information on:

* Orders
* Customers
* Products
* Categories
* Regions
* Sales
* Profit
* Quantity
* Discounts

## Key Insights

### 1. Sales Growth

Sales increased steadily between 2014 and 2017, indicating positive business growth.

### 2. Regional Performance

The West region generated the highest profit, followed by the East region.

### 3. Product Performance

Technology emerged as the highest-performing category by sales revenue.

### 4. Customer Analysis

A small group of customers contributed significantly to total revenue, highlighting the importance of customer retention.

### 5. Customer Segment Analysis

The Consumer segment accounted for over 50% of total sales.

---

## Recommendations

* Increase investment in high-performing technology products.
* Replicate successful sales strategies from the West region.
* Strengthen retention efforts for top customers.
* Improve performance in lower-profit regions.
* Expand engagement with Corporate and Home Office customer segments.
